## Descripción breve

Read, write with your finger and annotate PDFs. No accounts or ads.

## Descripción completa

Portylus is a free PDF reader and annotation app for Android.

Open documents with the Android file picker or from other apps. Add folders, bookmark locations and return to the last page of each PDF.

Write by hand with your finger or a compatible stylus, highlight, erase parts or entire strokes, undo and redo. Save three colour and width styles for each drawing tool. You can also select and copy existing PDF text.

The portrait phone layout keeps the tools visible. Settings let you choose Spanish, English or French, enable finger writing and keep the screen on. Use two fingers to pan and zoom while a drawing tool is active.

You choose when to save. Annotations are saved inside the PDF, and Save as lets you choose another destination. Portylus warns you before leaving with unsaved changes.

No accounts, ads or telemetry. PDFs are processed on your device and the app works offline. If you choose a cloud file provider, that provider manages file synchronisation.

Requires Android 13 or later. Text selection requires an existing text layer; OCR is not included. Password-protected PDFs and keyboard text annotation are not yet supported.

All features are free. Optional support on Ko-fi does not unlock content or benefits.
