## Título

Looking for testers: Portylus, an offline open-source Android PDF annotation app

## Mensaje

I’m preparing the closed test for Portylus, a free PDF reader and annotation app for Android 13 and later.

It supports handwriting with a finger or compatible stylus, highlighting, erasing strokes, undo/redo and saving annotations inside the PDF. It works offline, without accounts, ads or telemetry. The interface is available in Spanish, English and French.

I’m looking for feedback on portrait phone layouts, finger writing, language switching and saving/reopening PDFs across different phones and tablets. I’m interested in honest reports, not positive ratings.

Version 0.1.1 is prepared and uploaded as a draft to Google Play; the closed test is not live yet. If you would like to join, send me a private message or email gtavo.apps@gmail.com with your interest and Android device model. Once the test is available I’ll share the verified joining steps. Please don’t post your private Google email address publicly.

Public repository: https://github.com/gtavo/Portylus
