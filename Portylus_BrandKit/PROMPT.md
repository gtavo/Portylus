# Generación del símbolo

Herramienta: image_gen integrada. Fondo transparente. Activo final copiado al proyecto sin editar.
Prompt enviado:

Design the final production app brand SYMBOL for PDFiz, a polished minimalist native Android PDF reader and handwritten annotator for teachers. It belongs in the same visual family as Docenda and Positio: rich electric blue #087CFF and #005BFF, luminous cyan #20C9FF, white #F7FAFF, deep navy #071A33 used only as subtle dark details; soft generously rounded geometry, slight dimensional bevel and restrained gradient, premium clear silhouette. Create one distinctive bold uppercase P-shaped cobalt/cyan folded document symbol, with a clean white paper inset and a short diagonal stylus with a small warm amber #FFC857 tip to communicate manual annotation. A readable uppercase P silhouette is essential, do not make it a D or a generic Adobe Acrobat mark. The symbol should feel solid, orderly, approachable and crisp, never cartoonish. Focus on a single strong idea, minimal internal details, excellent readability at app-icon sizes. Isolated centered free-standing symbol on a genuinely transparent background, no rounded square container, no background tile, no mockup, no surrounding shadow outside symbol, no checkerboard, no text or wordmark except the abstract P shape itself. Keep all nontransparent artwork comfortably inside the central 78% of a square canvas with balanced whitespace. This is the real logo asset to ship, not a presentation or a contact sheet.
